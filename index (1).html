About

I build firmware where correctness is measured in microseconds and margins are measured in electrons. At SanDisk I shipped eMMC/UFS features for automotive NAND, built telemetry that turned reliability debugging from guesswork into data, and hold a U.S. patent on predictable low-latency access. Now I'm at UW pairing that industry depth with computer architecture, FPGA/RTL, and real-time systems — and this summer, working on telemetry and physical-storage algorithms for enterprise SSDs. On campus, I serve as Corporate Relations Representative on UW's IEEE Women in Engineering board, connecting students with industry.
